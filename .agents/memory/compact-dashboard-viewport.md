---
name: Compact dashboard viewport checks
description: Verify the daily reconciliation dashboard at its short reference viewport.
---

For the daily reconciliation dashboard, verify the dense three-column layout at 1024×457, not only at the default preview size. Confirm the central system-balance summary and the right-side reconciliation difference remain fully visible.

**Why:** The 1280×720 preview looked complete while the 1024×457 layout clipped the bottom of the central system-balance card.

**How to apply:** Before marking a compact dashboard mockup live, capture a browser screenshot at the supplied reference dimensions and adjust the short-height layout until all summary totals are visible.
